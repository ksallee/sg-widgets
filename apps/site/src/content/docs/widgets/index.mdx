Each widget page shows the Svelte and React implementations side by side. One control bar under the
header sets the framework, one of ten themes, the corner radius, light or dark and reduced motion for
every demo on the page, and those choices carry across pages.

Demos run on fixtures until you switch the bar to Live, give it your site and log in through your
site's own approval page. Your session stays in this browser, this site keeps nothing of it on a
server, and the project you pick in the bar is the one the demos that take a project read.

Widgets install from a registry into your project, one command per framework:

```sh
pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add https://sg-widgets.dev/r/react/status-badge.json
pnpm dlx shadcn-svelte@latest add https://sg-widgets.dev/r/svelte/status-badge.json
```

Every widget that reads takes a service from `@sg-widgets/core`, built once over your client: the
schema pickers take a `SchemaService`. Wire your own client, or the shipped REST one.
